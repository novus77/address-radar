On any failure or deadline, cease the isolated workload, confirm its owned cleanup, and restore exactly the previously active permitted units. Never start a unit that was inactive before maintenance merely to normalize the inventory.

A capacity pause is not a final writer-fence attestation. Even a successful rehearsal on the old immutable backup does not prove coverage of subsequent production writes.

### Final migration window

Requires the independently approved complete writer/input scope, V2 evidence, matching target and budgets, actual operator/deployment admission controls, signed maintenance window, and compatible PostgreSQL rollback release proof. Disable approved re-entry points before stopping their workers; verify child FD/mmap holders and bridged cursors; then take and validate the fresh consistent source snapshot. Restore and verify the complete snapshot before import. Use same-source exact counts, typed parity, foreign-key checks, generation/cohort checks, and actual receiver/consumer receipts.

Before the first PostgreSQL business write, a safe abort may preserve the verified SQLite source under the original authorization. After that first write, rollback must preserve the new PostgreSQL writes and use the exact approved PostgreSQL-compatible artifact; restarting the old SQLite writer chain is forbidden. Backup scheduling must follow the selected backend rather than silently reopening the old SQLite database.
